Data Deletion Instructions

To delete the data the Rawi Turkish Instagram automation holds about you:

  1. Send an email to YOUR_EMAIL with the subject "Data deletion".
  2. Include your Instagram username so we can find your records.
  3. We will delete your records (user ID, username, comment and message-log entries) and confirm by email.

You can also remove our app from Instagram under Settings ⇒ Apps and websites, and then email us to delete the stored records.

What we store is described in our Privacy Policy.
