Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy describes how the Rawi Turkish Instagram automation (the "Service"), operated for the Instagram account @rawi.turkish, handles data. The Service lets people receive a free Turkish-learning book by commenting a keyword on a specific Reel.

Data we process

  • Your Instagram-scoped user ID and username, provided by Meta when you comment on our Reel or message our account.
  • The ID of the Reel and of your comment. We check whether your comment contains the trigger keyword; we do not store the comment text.
  • Whether you follow our account, retrieved from Meta's API, to decide whether to send the book link.
  • Technical records of the messages we send you (message IDs, timestamps, status, error codes). We do not store the content of messages you send us.

Why we use it

Only to reply to your comment, check your follow status, and send you the book link. We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

Where it is stored

On Cloudflare (Workers and D1 database). Data is received from and sent through Meta's Instagram APIs.

Retention

We keep these records until you ask us to delete them (see below).

Your rights and deletion

You can ask us to delete your data at any time. See how to request deletion.
